Descriptions

In one line

Walk six dogs at once. Try not to get tangled.

Short

Walk six dogs at once. Try not to get tangled. You run a dog-walking business for a week of 3-minute shifts. One finger. Free to play. No ads.

Longer

You run a one-person dog-walking business for a week. Each day is a 3-minute shift on a top-down neighbourhood. You play with one finger, in portrait.

Walk past a house to clip its dog on. Each dog wants one spot. It might be the dog park or the pond. It might be the sniff garden or the treat stand. Take the dog there, then bring it home. That is one delivery.

Every delivery pays more with more dogs on the leash. One dog pays ×1. Six dogs pay ×5. A bigger pack also knots faster.

Leashes wrap round lamp posts and trees. Leashes twist round each other too. Three twists and a dog sits down, stuck. Draw a small circle to twirl. A twirl undoes a twist.

Key features

  • A week of 3-minute shifts. Seven days on a top-down neighbourhood, played with one finger, in portrait.
  • Six dogs, six personalities. Hunter, Aspen, Honey, Domino, Pepper and Dash. Every dog warns you a beat before it acts. Six more breeds wait to be unlocked.
  • More dogs, more pay. One dog pays ×1. Six dogs pay ×5.
  • Wraps and twists. Leashes wrap round lamp posts and trees and twist round each other. Every twist shows a number. Three twists and a dog sits down, stuck.
  • Scooping. Dogs sometimes leave a mess. Walk over it to scoop it. You carry up to 3 bags, and a bin pays a tip. A mess left 40 seconds costs a fine.
  • A growing neighbourhood. It grows through the week, from a small park to a long street the camera follows.
  • 16 upgrades. Pick 1 of 3 between days: 8 gear, 4 tricks and 4 big clients.
  • Six special days. Thunderstorm, squirrel festival, dog show, rush hour, fog and moving day, on days 6 and 7.
  • Four ways to play. Week, Daily Shift with a Game Center leaderboard, Endless Week and the Kennel. Three more neighbourhoods wait to be unlocked: Harbour, Old Town and Suburbs.
  • Easy to read. Every leash has its own colour and its own pattern. VoiceOver reads a label for every dog. You can reduce motion, switch to a left-handed mode and play in the Day or Dusk theme.
  • Free, then one price. The free game is a real game: the Daily Shift, the career up to Pack Leader, the seasonal event, unlimited replays, 18 breeds and the Parkside neighbourhood. Walkies Pro is a single $2.99 purchase that adds three ranks, all 37 breeds, three more neighbourhoods and Endless Week. It sells content, never an advantage. No ads, no subscription, no accounts, and no data collected.
