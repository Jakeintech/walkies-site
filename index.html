Walkies: Dog Walker Arcade

Walk six dogs at once. Try not to get tangled.

You run a one-person dog-walking business for a week. Each day is a 3-minute shift on a top-down neighbourhood. You play with one finger, in portrait.

How it plays

Clip on. Walk. Bring it home.

  1. Step 1

    Clip on

    Walk past a house to clip its dog on.

  2. Step 2

    Find its spot

    Each dog wants one spot. It might be the dog park or the pond. It might be the sniff garden or the treat stand.

  3. Step 3

    Bring it home

    Take the dog there, then bring it home. That is one delivery.

More dogs, more pay

Every delivery pays more with more dogs on the leash. One dog pays ×1. Six dogs pay ×5. A bigger pack also knots faster.

1 dog×1 up to 6 dogs×5

The catch

Leashes wrap. Leashes twist.

  • Wrap

    Leashes wrap round lamp posts and trees. Walk back round the post to unwrap.

  • Twist

    Leashes twist round each other too. Every twist shows a number. Three twists and a dog sits down, stuck.

  • Twirl

    Draw a small circle to twirl. A twirl undoes a twist.

  • Scoop

    Dogs sometimes leave a mess. Walk over it to scoop it. You carry up to 3 bags. A bin pays a tip. A mess left 40 seconds costs a fine.

A growing neighbourhood

The neighbourhood grows through the week. It starts as a small park. It ends as a long street. The camera follows you.

Four ways to play

One week, and then some.

  • Week

    Seven days, one business.

  • Daily Shift

    One seeded day, the same for everyone. You get one attempt. Scores go to a Game Center leaderboard.

  • Endless Week

    Keep walking after day 7. The targets keep growing.

  • The Kennel

    Every dog you have walked home 5 times or more.

Three more neighbourhoods wait to be unlocked. They are Harbour, Old Town and Suburbs.

For every player

Easy to read.

  • VoiceOver reads a label for every dog.
