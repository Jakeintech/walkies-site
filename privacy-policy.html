Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Walkies collects no data.

  • No accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • Your progress is stored on your iPhone.
  • The only network feature is Apple's Game Center, for the Daily Shift leaderboard. It is optional, and Apple runs it under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Walkies is not released yet. This policy applies from the day it is.

1. Who we are

Walkies is a game for iPhone made by Jake Williams ("we", "us"). Its name on the App Store is Walkies: Dog Walker Arcade. This policy explains what information the game handles, where it goes and the choices you have. Questions: info@jakeawilliams.com.

2. Scope

This policy covers the Walkies game for iPhone and this website. It does not cover Apple's own services, such as the App Store and Game Center. Those are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Our core privacy posture

  • No accounts. Walkies has no sign-up, no username and no password. We do not know who you are.
  • No servers. Walkies has no server of its own. The game sends us nothing.
  • No third-party SDKs. The game contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software from anyone.
  • No tracking. Walkies does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • No advertising. Walkies shows no ads.
  • No in-app purchases. You pay once, up front.
  • No sale, no sharing. We hold no personal information about you, so there is none to sell or share.

4. What the game handles

5. Game Center

Game Center is used only for the Daily Shift leaderboard, and it is optional. Apple runs Game Center and processes that information under its own privacy policy. If you use it, your score appears on the leaderboard under your Game Center name. You control your Game Center profile in Settings > Game Center on your iPhone.

6. Third-party services

Apple is the only other party involved: the App Store for the purchase, and Game Center for the leaderboard. Both are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Walkies uses no other third-party service.

7. This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Its typeface, Inter Tight, is loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for the font files. Google's Privacy Policy covers that request.

8. Children

Walkies collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Game Center is controlled by Apple and can be restricted by a parent with Screen Time. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Walkies keeps your progress on your own iPhone, so you can exercise these rights directly by deleting the game. We do not sell or share personal information. Nothing is tracked, so Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals need nothing further from us. If you have a request we can help with, write to info@jakeawilliams.com.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

Your progress stays on your iPhone until you delete the game, which removes everything Walkies stored. We hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

To delete it, touch and hold Walkies on the Home Screen, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Copies inside your own device backups follow the rules of that backup. Scores recorded in Game Center are managed by Apple.

11. Security

Walkies relies on the protections built into iOS: its saved progress sits inside the app's own storage on your iPhone.

12. International transfers

The game sends us no personal data, so we transfer none to another country.

13. App Store privacy label

Walkies is not released yet. We expect its privacy label on the App Store to read Data Not Collected, which matches this policy. If you ever find the two disagree, tell us and we will correct it.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
