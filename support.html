Release

When is Walkies out?

Not yet. Walkies is coming soon to the App Store. There is no date to share.

Which devices does it run on?

Walkies is for iPhone. It requires iOS 18 or later. You play in portrait.

Playing

How does a shift work?

A shift is 3 minutes on a top-down neighbourhood. Walk past a house to clip its dog on. Take the dog to the spot it wants. Then bring it home. That is one delivery.

Where do dogs want to go?

Each dog wants one spot. It might be the dog park or the pond. It might be the sniff garden or the treat stand.

How does pay work?

Every delivery pays more with more dogs on the leash. One dog pays ×1. Six dogs pay ×5. A bigger pack also knots faster.

A leash is wrapped round a post. What now?

Leashes wrap round lamp posts and trees. Walk back round the post to unwrap.

My dog sat down and won't move.

Its leash has three twists. Every twist shows a number. Three twists and a dog sits down, stuck. Draw a small circle to twirl. A twirl undoes a twist.

A dog left a mess. What do I do?

Walk over it to scoop it. You carry up to 3 bags. A bin pays a tip. A mess left 40 seconds costs a fine.

What happens between days?

You pick 1 of 3 upgrades. There are 16: 8 gear, 4 tricks and 4 big clients.

Does the map change?

Yes. The neighbourhood grows through the week. It starts as a small park. It ends as a long street. The camera follows you.

What are special days?

Days 6 and 7 are special days. There are six kinds. Thunderstorm, squirrel festival and dog show. Rush hour, fog and moving day.

How does the week end early?

Each day has a target to earn. Three strikes close the business.

Who are the dogs?

You start with six dogs. Hunter is a Sniffer. Aspen is Social. Honey is a Puller. Domino is a Chaser. Pepper is a Slowpoke. Dash is a Zoomie. Six more breeds wait to be unlocked. Meet the dogs

Modes

What is the Daily Shift?

One seeded day, the same for everyone. You get one attempt. Scores go to a Game Center leaderboard.

Do I need Game Center?

No. Game Center is optional. Walkies only uses it for the Daily Shift leaderboard. Apple runs it. You manage it in Settings > Game Center on your iPhone.

What is Endless Week?

Keep walking after day 7. The targets keep growing.

What is the Kennel?

Every dog you have walked home 5 times or more.

Are there more neighbourhoods?

Yes. Three more wait to be unlocked. They are Harbour, Old Town and Suburbs.

Accessibility

Do I need to tell colours apart?

No. Every leash has its own colour and its own pattern. Colour is never the only signal.

Does it work with VoiceOver?

VoiceOver reads a label for every dog.

What else can I change?

You can reduce motion. You can switch to a left-handed mode. You can play in the Day or Dusk theme.

Saves

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone. Walkies has no accounts, so we hold no copy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the game. Touch and hold Walkies on the Home Screen. Tap Remove App, then Delete App. We hold no copy, so nothing else needs deleting. The privacy policy has more.

Purchase and privacy

What does Walkies cost?

Nothing to download, and the free game is a real game: the Daily Shift, the career up to Pack Leader, the seasonal event, unlimited replays, 18 breeds and the Parkside neighbourhood. If you want the rest, Walkies Pro is one purchase, once — three more ranks, all 37 breeds, three more neighbourhoods and Endless Week. It is shared with your family, and it never expires.

Are there ads or subscriptions?

No ads, ever, and no subscription. There is one optional in-app purchase, Walkies Pro, bought once. It adds content — it never makes a shift easier or a score higher. There are no accounts either.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What data does Walkies collect?

None. Walkies has no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. The privacy policy has the detail.
